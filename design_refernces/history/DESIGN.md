## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a mission-critical, enterprise computer vision and telematics aesthetic. Inspired by high-precision software like Linear, Stripe, and modern deep-learning observation desks, it blends a deep carbon canvas with crisp, electric functional accents.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Surgical Precision:** Every pixel, border, and badge serves an analytical purpose. Clutter is stripped away to facilitate instant triage of highway inspection data.
- **Controlled Velocity:** Dynamic high-contrast accents (sharp lime and electric blue) cut through pitch-black backgrounds, directing human-in-the-loop operators to road hazards without visual fatigue.
- **Enterprise Gravitas:** Dark slate surfaces and subtle boundaries project stability, technical depth, and security for DOTs, municipal engineers, and autonomous fleet planners.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Utilitarian Dark Mode:** Grounded on an ultra-deep palette (`#090A0F`), utilizing low-contrast surface elevation stepped through `#14151B` and `#18181C`.
- **Subtle Surface Definition:** Highlighting is achieved through hairline borders (`#2B2D35`) and selective glow ramps rather than heavy shadows.
- **Domain Specialization:** Built-in semantic treatments tailored for real-time asset classification: D00 Longitudinal Cracking, D10 Transverse Cracking, D20 Alligator Cracking, and D40 Pothole telemetry.

## Colors

The palette relies on a pitch-black canvas with layered charcoal tiers and high-energy analytical accents.

### Core Foundation
- **Canvas Base (`#090A0F`):** Main viewport background, providing maximum contrast for telemetry data and spatial vision overlays.
- **Sidebar & Structural Shelves (`#14151B`):** Dedicated navigation drawers, persistent toolbars, and global frame elements.
- **Surface Elevation (`#18181C`):** Primary card modules, data widgets, and camera feed containers.
- **Structural Outlines (`#2B2D35`):** 1px borders providing structure across cards, inputs, and chart grids.

### Key Accents & Semantics
- **Primary Accent — Volt Lime (`#E0EA6C`):** Key metric indicators, active KPI highlights, primary interactive triggers, and high-confidence AI detections.
- **Secondary Accent — Electric Blue (`#6989FF`):** Secondary pathways, auxiliary series on multi-axis charts, navigation active states, and system-level telemetry.
- **Telemetry Severity Palette:**
  - `D00 Longitudinal Crack`: Electric Blue (`#6989FF`)
  - `D10 Transverse Crack`: Cyan Azure (`#38BDF8`)
  - `D20 Alligator Crack`: Amber Warning (`#F59E0B`)
  - `D40 Pothole / Critical Structural Breach`: Crimson Alert (`#FF5C5C`)

### Typography Colors
- **Primary Readout (`#F5F5F5`):** Metric values, primary titles, critical timestamps, and object IDs.
- **Muted Readout (`#767881`):** Metric labels, secondary telemetry, chart grid ticks, and table column headers.
- **Inverse Canvas (`#090A0F`):** Text resting directly on top of filled `#E0EA6C` buttons and badges.

## Typography

The type hierarchy uses Lato across all interfaces, capitalizing on its structural warmth and clear vertical proportions to maintain legibility in dense data layouts.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Monospaced Figures:** All numeric displays (GPS coordinates, defect dimensions, confidence rates, lane indices) must render with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to avoid layout jitter during live updates.
- **Labels & Damage Categorization:** Operational categories (`D00`, `D10`, `D20`, `D40`) use `label-sm` with uppercase styling and letter spacing (`0.05em`) for immediate recognition at rapid scan speeds.
- **Display Weights:** Large numeric cards and analytics callouts pair a bold weight (`700`) with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to command focal attention within dark containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a modular, fluid dashboard grid designed to house camera viewports, spatial maps, and analytics panels simultaneously.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with a fixed 72px icon sidebar or collapsible 240px enterprise drawer. Card gutters are fixed at `1rem` (16px), with canvas outer margins at `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Camera previews collapse to top-stack or persistent split-screen; widgets reflow to a 2-column format.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack. Sidebar transitions to an off-canvas drawer or bottom navigation rail. Margins step down to `1rem` (16px) with `0.75rem` (12px) component spacing.

### Density & Rhythms
- Built on a strict 4px/8px modular scale.
- Data grids and visual inference metadata panels favor compact density (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize spatial visibility on GIS viewports and video timelines.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using micro-borders and subtle luminescence to convey depth.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#090A0F`):** Deepest stratum. Houses raw GIS maps, imagery backdrops, and video streams.
- **Navigation Rails & Docking Bars (`#14151B`):** Resting surface with a 1px border (`#2B2D35`) on adjoining edges.
- **Surface Panels & Analytics Cards (`#18181C`):** Standard container surface, bounded by a uniform 1px solid `#2B2D35` border.
- **Interactive Modals & Overlays (`#202127`):** Elevated float layer featuring an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` and a high-definition border (`#3B3E4A`).

### Glow Accents & Bounding Highlights
- Critical anomalies (e.g., severe D40 Pothole) utilize a localized ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px 0 rgba(224, 234, 108, 0.15)`.
- Interactive hover transitions gently boost the border tone from `#2B2D35` to `#3E424E` without shifting card geometry.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced roundedness (Level 2), softening rigid engineering data screens while maintaining precise alignment.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Table Rows):** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Canvas Containers & Video Frames:** Medium radius of `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) with hidden overflow to seamlessly clip geospatial maps and visual feeds.
- **Detection Tags & Categorical Badges:** Pill-style corners (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata tags from structural, rectangular data cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#E0EA6C`, text `#090A0F` with bold weight (`700`). Interactive hover increases brightness subtly without modifying hue. Focus state exhibits an outer ring `0 0 0 2px #090A0F, 0 0 0 4px #E0EA6C`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#18181C`, border 1px solid `#2B2D35`, text `#F5F5F5`. Hover changes background to `#222329` and border to `#3B3E4A`.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill, text `#767881`, hover text `#F5F5F5`, and background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Chips & Semantic Tags
- **Defect Badges:** Compact height (22px), `label-sm` font, rounded-full.
  - *D00 Longitudinal Crack:* Background `rgba(105, 137, 255, 0.12)`, border `rgba(105, 137, 255, 0.4)`, text `#6989FF`.
  - *D10 Transverse Crack:* Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`, text `#38BDF8`.
  - *D20 Alligator Crack:* Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`, text `#F59E0B`.
  - *D40 Pothole:* Background `rgba(255, 92, 92, 0.15)`, border `rgba(255, 92, 92, 0.5)`, text `#FF5C5C`.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Card base: Solid `#18181C` with 1px border `#2B2D35` and `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Internal padding: `1.25rem` (20px). Card headers use `headline-sm` with text `#F5F5F5`, accompanied by right-aligned filter toggles or dropdowns styled in ghost mode.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Input fields: Background `#14151B`, border 1px solid `#2B2D35`, text `#F5F5F5`, placeholder `#767881`.
- Integrated search bar features a leading subtle search icon in `#767881` that transitions to `#E0EA6C` on active focus.

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- Detection stream rows: Alternating row highlights with transparent background, shifting to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` on hover.
- Row separators: 1px border `#2B2D35`. Each record pairs a confidence meter bar with a timestamp and visual crop modal trigger.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 18px square (or circle for radio) with background `#14151B` and 1px border `#2B2D35`.
- Selected: Background `#E0EA6C`, border `#E0EA6C`, icon checkmark `#090A0F`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Vision Bounding Overlay:** Canvas overlays render 1.5px vector rectangles over defects (e.g. Lime for general anomalies, Crimson for D40). Overlays feature a top-left corner label pill with an opacity fill of 85%.
- **Confidence Rating Bar:** A 4px tall horizontal bar; filled with `#E0EA6C` for >85% confidence, `#6989FF` for 70–85%, and `#767881` for uncertain passes.
